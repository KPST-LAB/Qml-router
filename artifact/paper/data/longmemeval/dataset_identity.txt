Classes used in the paper: multi-session (arm msf) and temporal-reasoning (arm trf), 133 questions each.
Per question: the haystack sessions are ingested as one qml session (lme-<arm>::<question_id>) and the
question is asked with the reader prompt below; the answer is judged with the official LongMemEval
judge prompts (paperjudge mode).

reader system prompt:
  <|begin_of_text|><|start_header_id|>system<|end_header_id|>\n\nYou are a helpful assistant. Answer briefly and directly.<|eot_id|>
question wrapper:
  <|eot_id|><|start_header_id|>user<|end_header_id|>\n\n{question}<|eot_id|><|start_header_id|>assistant<|end_header_id|>\n\n

files:
  results_<arm>_final.json              per-question model answer (question_id, hypothesis, injected tokens)
  results_<arm>_final_paperjudged.json  the same rows with the official judge verdict
  answers_<arm>.json                    per question, token ranges of the gold sessions inside the ingested archive
